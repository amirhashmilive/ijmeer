01 Law / Political Science

Implementation of the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006 in Rural Rajasthan: An Empirical Evaluation of Institutional Deficiencies and Judicial Responses

Dr. Ramesh Kumar & Sunita Sharma

Despite comprehensive legislative prohibition under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act (PCMA) 2006, child marriage remains prevalent in several rural pockets of Rajasthan. This paper presents an empirical evaluation of the operational effectiveness of Child Marriage Prohibition Officers (CMPOs), grassroots law enforcement challenges, and judicial responses across three districts (Tonk, Sawai Madhopur, and Baran). Utilizing court case data, CMPO performance metrics, and structured interviews with 18 stakeholders, the study highlights critical institutional deficiencies including multi-tasking CMPO roles without dedicated budget, procedural delays in obtaining ex parte injunctions, and persistent conflicts between personal laws and the PCMA. The study outlines targeted policy recommendations to strengthen enforcement mechanisms and community legal awareness.

02 Public Health / Community Medicine

Prevalence and Determinants of Anaemia Among Adolescent Girls in Tribal Districts of Chhattisgarh: A Cross-Sectional Study

Dr. Sunita Verma & Kavita Sahu

Anaemia remains a persistent public health burden in India, disproportionately affecting adolescent girls in tribal and rural populations. This cross-sectional study was conducted across three tribal-majority districts of Chhattisgarh — Bastar, Narayanpur, and Bijapur — to assess the prevalence and determinants of anaemia among adolescent girls aged 10 to 19 years. A total of 450 participants were enrolled through cluster sampling from government school and Anganwadi settings. Blood haemoglobin levels were measured using a calibrated digital haemoglobinometer, and a validated structured questionnaire gathered data on dietary habits, menstrual patterns, household socioeconomic characteristics, and awareness of the POSHAN Abhiyaan. The study found an overall anaemia prevalence of 67.6% by WHO haemoglobin cut-offs. Findings underscore the urgent need for targeted nutritional interventions, improved IFA distribution mechanisms, and community-level health education in Chhattisgarh's tribal districts.

03 Economics / Finance

Digital Financial Inclusion and Financial Literacy Among Rural Women: Evidence from Self-Help Group Members in North Odisha

Dr. Anupam Sahoo & Dr. Bijay Kumar Swain

Digital financial services have expanded dramatically in rural India under the PMJDY and UPI ecosystems, yet gender disparities in digital financial literacy persist. This empirical study evaluates the extent of digital financial inclusion and financial literacy among 320 women Self-Help Group (SHG) members across Mayurbhanj and Balasore districts of North Odisha. Using a multidimensional financial literacy index encompassing digital knowledge, usage frequency, and risk perception, the study finds that while account ownership is near-universal (94.4%), active digital usage remains low (31.2%). Regression analysis reveals that educational attainment, smartphone ownership, and SHG training frequency are significant positive determinants of digital financial competency.

04 Psychology / Education

Academic Stress and Coping Strategies Among Undergraduate Students of Arts Colleges in Marathwada Region, Maharashtra

Dr. Meena Kulkarni

Academic stress is a major psychological concern affecting higher education students in India, influenced by examination pressure, career uncertainty, and socio-economic expectations. This study investigates the sources of academic stress and coping mechanisms among 280 undergraduate arts students across three degree colleges in Marathwada region, Maharashtra. Utilizing the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) and the Brief COPE inventory, the study identified examination burden and future employability as primary stressors. Problem-focused coping strategies were associated with lower stress levels, whereas avoidance coping correlated with higher anxiety.

05 Agriculture / Environmental Science

Impact of Organic Farming Practices on Soil Health and Crop Yield in Small Landholdings of Western Uttar Pradesh

Dr. Ramesh Yadav & Manju Devi

Long-term chemical fertilizer application has led to soil organic carbon depletion and declining micro-nutrient availability in the intensive agricultural belt of Western Uttar Pradesh. This study assesses the comparative impact of organic farming practices (vermicompost, green manuring, and bio-fertilizers) versus conventional chemical farming on soil health parameters and crop yield over three cropping cycles among 120 smallholder farmers in Muzaffarnagar and Meerut districts. Results show significant improvements in soil organic carbon (SOC), microbial biomass nitrogen, and water retention capacity in organic plots.

06 Environmental Science / Limnology

Water Quality Assessment of Urban Lakes in Bhopal: A Physico-Chemical Study

Dr. Shyam Sundar Patel

Urbanization, untreated sewage discharge, and anthropogenic pressures threaten freshwater ecosystems in Central India. This study evaluated the physico-chemical water quality parameters of Upper Lake (Bada Talab) and Lower Lake (Chhota Talab) in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, across pre-monsoon, monsoon, and post-monsoon seasons. Parameters including pH, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD), Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), nitrate, phosphate, and heavy metals were analyzed. Lower Lake exhibited severe eutrophication and high BOD levels exceeding CPCB standards.

07 Education / Pedagogy

Effectiveness of Activity-Based Learning in Improving Literacy Outcomes in Primary Schools of Backward Districts in Odisha

Sudha Panda & Dr. Bijaya Kumar Sahoo

Activity-Based Learning (ABL) pedagogy has been adopted across several Indian states to foster child-centered learning and reduce foundational learning deficits. This quasi-experimental study evaluated the efficacy of ABL intervention on reading comprehension, vocabulary, and numeracy skills among 360 Grade 3 and 5 students in primary schools across Sambalpur and Kandhamal districts of Odisha. Pre- and post-test scores demonstrated statistically significant improvements in student learning outcomes in ABL classrooms compared to traditional rote-learning control groups.

10 Management / Commerce

Corporate Social Responsibility Practices of Small and Medium Enterprises in Tirunelveli District, Tamil Nadu: An Empirical Investigation

Dr. K. Muthuvel

While Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) is legislatively mandated for large corporations under Section 135 of the Indian Companies Act 2013, Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) frequently practice informal, community-oriented CSR. This study empirically investigates the CSR engagement patterns, motivations, and operational barriers among 150 SMEs in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu. Results indicate that 43.3% of SMEs engage in philanthropic CSR activities, with community development and local educational support being primary focus areas.

11 Computer Science / Precision Agriculture

Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Algorithms for Crop Disease Detection Using Leaf Image Data

Dr. Anoop Kumar Sharma & Dr. James Osei

Timely and accurate detection of crop diseases is critical for minimising agricultural yield losses and reducing the overuse of chemical pesticides. This study conducted a comparative performance evaluation of three machine learning approaches — Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), Support Vector Machine (SVM), and Random Forest (RF) — for the automatic detection and classification of nine common crop diseases across four crop species using leaf image data drawn from the PlantVillage dataset (54,306 images). The fine-tuned CNN architecture achieved the highest overall classification accuracy of 94.2%, outperforming SVM (87.1%) and RF (82.3%).

12 Physics / Materials Science

Synthesis and Characterisation of Zinc Oxide Nanoparticles by Chemical Precipitation Method and Investigation of Optical Properties

Dr. Fatima Begum & Dr. Nguyen Van Minh

Zinc Oxide (ZnO) nanoparticles possess exceptional optoelectronic properties making them suitable for solar cells, UV photodetectors, and photocatalytic applications. In this study, ZnO nanoparticles were synthesized via a cost-effective chemical precipitation route using zinc acetate dihydrate and sodium hydroxide precursors. Structural characterization via X-ray Diffraction (XRD) confirmed a hexagonal wurtzite crystal structure with an average crystallite size of 24.5 nm. UV-Visible spectroscopy revealed a direct optical band gap of 3.28 eV.

13 Chemistry / Nanotechnology

Green Synthesis of Silver Nanoparticles Using Hibiscus rosa-sinensis Leaf Extract and Evaluation of Antibacterial Activity

Dr. Mary Joseph & Sr. Anitha Thomas

Eco-friendly bio-synthesis of metallic nanoparticles using plant extracts offers significant advantages over hazardous chemical synthesis routes. This study demonstrates the rapid green synthesis of silver nanoparticles (AgNPs) using aqueous leaf extract of Hibiscus rosa-sinensis as both reducing and capping agent. Formation of AgNPs was confirmed by UV-Vis surface plasmon resonance peak at 425 nm. Synthesized AgNPs exhibited potent antibacterial activity against Gram-negative (E. coli, P. aeruginosa) and Gram-positive (S. aureus) bacterial strains.

14 English Literature / Cultural Studies

Postcolonial Identity and Hybrid Consciousness in Arundhati Roy's The Ministry of Utmost Happiness

Dr. Deepa Rani

Arundhati Roy's second novel, The Ministry of Utmost Happiness (2017), offers a profound engagement with postcolonial nationhood, marginality, and hybrid identities in contemporary India. Applying Homi Bhabha's concepts of 'third space', 'hybridity', and 'mimicry', this paper analyzes how Roy subverts monolithic nationalistic discourses through marginal characters such as Anjum, a hijra who establishes a sanctuary in a Delhi graveyard, and Tilo, an architecture student caught in the turmoil of Kashmir. The study demonstrates how Roy's fragmented narrative structure mirrors the contested geopolitical landscapes of postcolonial South Asia.

01 Law / Political Science

Implementation of Three New Criminal Laws in India: A Study of Police Preparedness in Uttar Pradesh

Arjun Singh

The enactment of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023, and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023, which replaced the Indian Penal Code, the Code of Criminal Procedure, and the Indian Evidence Act respectively, marks the most comprehensive overhaul of India's criminal justice system since independence. This paper examines the state of preparedness among the Uttar Pradesh Police—India's largest state police force—in implementing these transformative legislations. The study reveals significant gaps in awareness, infrastructure, and inter-agency coordination, recommending a phased capacity-building approach and enhanced digital infrastructure.

Impact of MSME Schemes on Women Entrepreneurs in Tamil Nadu: Evidence from Madurai and Tirunelveli Districts

Meena Rajan

Women entrepreneurs in India's MSME sector remain significantly underrepresented. This paper examines the impact of various government schemes on women-led enterprises in Madurai and Tirunelveli districts.
